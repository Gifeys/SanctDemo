SanctiWalk

A guide to the parishes of the Diocese of Kalookan. Mass times, the story of each church, and an answer when you are standing in front of something and wondering what it is.

Installing it

  1. Tap Download for Android above and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Your phone will warn you that it came from outside the Play Store — that warning is normal for a student project, and you only have to allow it once.
  3. Tap Install, then open SanctiWalk and sign in with Google so your scans and applications are saved to your account.

What it looks like

What it does

Tells you when Mass is
Kept current by the parish office itself, not by us. When they change a time, it changes on your phone.
Explains what you are looking at
Point the camera at a statue, an altar or an image and it will name it and tell you who they were. Twenty a day, free.
Reads the church's history aloud
In English or Filipino, so you can listen while you walk rather than read with your head down.
Prays the Rosary with you
All four sets of mysteries, in both languages, with a guided voice if you want one. Works with no signal.
Walks you there
Real street directions to any parish in the diocese, following roads rather than drawing a line across the map.

Before you install

  • Android 8 or later. Everything works, including the AR tour.
  • iPhone: use the web version above. It does everything except the AR tour, which needs Android.
  • The AR tour is a separate app and needs a phone that supports Google ARCore.
  • Offline: Mass times, church history and the Rosary work with no signal. The camera scanner needs internet.
  • Permissions: the camera is used only while you are scanning, and the frame is analysed and discarded — nothing is kept. Location is used to tell which parish you are at.

Built as a capstone project for STI College, in consultation with Mary Help of Christians Parish and San Roque Cathedral Parish.
